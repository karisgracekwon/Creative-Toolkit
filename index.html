Creative Toolkit

Practical ways to build creativity into everyday learning

Creative thinking plays a major role in problem-solving, communication, and long-term adaptability. Many students have fewer chances to use those skills during the school day. This toolkit provides short, flexible activities that support creative growth within existing classes and routines.

Created as part of a student civic engagement project
