How to Use These Activities

  • Use once or twice per week to maintain novelty
  • Keep grading low-stakes or participation-based
  • Encourage multiple approaches and interpretations
  • Set short time limits to keep energy focused

Classroom Benefits

  • Increases student engagement and attention
  • Encourages flexible thinking
  • Supports collaboration and discussion
  • Provides a mental reset during long class periods
